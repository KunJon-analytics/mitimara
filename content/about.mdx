## How {siteName} Works

{siteName} operates on a simple yet powerful model that connects tree planting with verification, community oversight, and rewards:

### The Planting Process

1. **Plant a Tree**: Users plant real trees in their communities and document the planting with geotagged photos.
2. **Community Verification**: Nearby community members physically verify the tree's existence and condition.
3. **Earn Rewards**: Successfully verified trees generate points that can be exchanged for Pi cryptocurrency.
4. **Ongoing Monitoring**: Trees can be revisited and updated, creating a living record of growth and impact.

### Our Dual-Point System

{siteName}'s ecosystem is powered by two types of points that incentivize different positive behaviors:

- **Ecosystem Points**: Earned through planting and verifying trees, these points reward the primary activities that grow our forest network.
- **Policing Points**: Earned through community oversight activities, these points ensure the integrity of our platform by rewarding those who help maintain trust and accuracy.

Both point types can be exchanged for Pi tokens, creating real economic value for environmental contributions.

### Community Policing

To maintain the highest standards of integrity, {siteName} incorporates a robust community policing system:

- Users can report suspicious tree claims for review
- Contested verifications undergo administrative evaluation
- Honest reporting is rewarded with additional points
- Dishonest behavior results in point penalties

This self-regulating system ensures that {siteName} remains a trusted platform where genuine environmental action is recognized and rewarded.

### Accessing {siteName}

{siteName} is accessed exclusively through the Pi Browser app, which is essential for our platform's functionality:

- **Pi Browser Integration**: Users access {siteName} through the Pi Browser app, which provides the necessary authentication with the Pi blockchain
- **Secure Payments**: This integration enables secure Pi token transactions, which are core to our reward system
- **Blockchain Authentication**: The Pi blockchain authentication ensures transparent and verifiable record-keeping of all activities

This approach allows us to leverage the security and functionality of the Pi Network while creating a seamless experience for users.
