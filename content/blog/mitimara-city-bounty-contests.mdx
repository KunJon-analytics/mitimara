# {siteName} City Bounty Contests: Turning Local Action into Global Impact

## Introduction: Tree Planting Gets Competitive

Climate change is a global challenge, but addressing it often starts with local action. At {siteName}, we believe in the power of community-driven environmental initiatives, which is why we're excited to introduce **City Bounty Contests** – a feature that transforms tree planting into a friendly, rewarding competition between neighborhoods, cities, and regions.

But these aren't just any contests – they're strategically designed challenges that direct tree-planting efforts where they're needed most, creating a win-win scenario for communities and the environment.

## What Are City Bounty Contests?

City Bounty Contests are time-limited, location-specific challenges that encourage concentrated tree planting efforts in particular geographic areas. These contests operate as a layer on top of {siteName}'s core tree-planting activities, adding extra incentives for planting trees in designated locations during specific timeframes.

Each bounty contest has several key components:

- **Geographic Boundary**: A clearly defined area where trees must be planted to qualify
- **Time Limit**: A specific start and end date for the contest
- **Reward Pool**: A dedicated pot of Pi tokens allocated specifically for participants
- **Participation Requirements**: Clear guidelines on what constitutes a qualifying tree planting
- **Verification Process**: Enhanced verification to ensure contest integrity

## How City Bounty Contests Work

### Contest Creation

Bounty contests can be created by:

1. **{siteName} Team**: We regularly launch global contests targeting high-priority reforestation areas.
2. **Local Governments**: Cities and municipalities can sponsor contests to increase their green coverage.
3. **Community Leaders**: Verified community members with sufficient [policing points](/blog/mitimara-dual-point-system) can propose and organize local contests.
4. **Environmental Organizations**: Partner NGOs can sponsor targeted planting initiatives.

### Participating in a Contest

Joining a bounty contest is simple:

1. **Find Active Contests**: Browse the "Bounty Hunts" section in your {siteName} app to see contests in your area.
2. **Join a Contest**: Opt into any contests that interest you – there's no limit to how many you can join!
3. **Plant Trees in the Zone**: [Plant trees](/blog/how-to-plant-a-tree) within the designated geographic boundary during the contest period.
4. **Verification**: Your trees will be verified through our [standard community verification process](/blog/how-to-verify-a-tree), with added location verification.
5. **Earn Rewards**: Successfully verified trees count toward both your regular rewards AND the contest rewards.

### Reward Distribution

Contest rewards are distributed based on a participation-weighted formula:

```plaintext
Your Contest Reward = (Your Verified Trees in Contest / Total Verified Trees in Contest) * Contest Reward Pool
```

This ensures that rewards are fairly distributed based on actual contribution, with larger rewards going to those who contribute more to the contest's success.

## Types of City Bounty Contests

We offer several different contest formats to address various environmental and community needs:

### 1. Reforestation Challenges

Focused on areas suffering from deforestation or natural disasters, these contests direct planting efforts where ecological restoration is most urgent.

### 2. Urban Greening Initiatives

Targeting urban areas with low tree coverage, these contests help combat urban heat islands and improve air quality in cities.

### 3. Biodiversity Boosts

These specialized contests focus on planting specific native tree species to support local biodiversity and ecosystem health.

### 4. Community Competitions

Friendly competitions between neighboring communities, schools, or organizations to see who can plant the most trees.

### 5. Seasonal Sprints

Short-duration, high-intensity contests that coincide with optimal planting seasons in specific regions.

## Benefits of City Bounty Contests

### For Participants

- **Increased Rewards**: Earn both standard {siteName} rewards AND contest-specific rewards
- **Community Recognition**: Top contributors receive badges and leaderboard recognition
- **Local Impact**: See the direct impact of your efforts in your own community
- **Skill Development**: Learn about local ecology and proper tree planting techniques

### For Communities

- **Targeted Environmental Improvement**: Trees planted where they're needed most
- **Community Engagement**: Increased participation in local environmental initiatives
- **Educational Opportunities**: Learning about local ecosystems and climate action
- **Beautification**: More trees means more beautiful, livable communities

### For the Environment

- **Strategic Reforestation**: Directing efforts to high-priority ecological areas
- **Biodiversity Support**: Encouraging planting of diverse, native species
- **Urban Climate Mitigation**: Reducing urban heat islands and improving air quality
- **Accelerated Impact**: Concentrating planting efforts for more significant ecological benefits

## How to Create Your Own Bounty Contest

Are you a community leader interested in creating a bounty contest for your area? Here's how:

1. **Build Your Reputation**: Accumulate sufficient policing points to demonstrate your commitment to platform integrity
2. **Identify a Need**: Research and document where tree planting efforts are most needed in your community
3. **Create a Proposal**: Use our Bounty Creator tool to outline your contest boundaries, timeline, and goals
4. **Secure Funding**: Allocate Pi tokens to the reward pool (or seek sponsorship from local organizations)
5. **Launch and Promote**: Once approved, spread the word in your community to maximize participation

## How City Bounty Contests Integrate with {siteName}'s Ecosystem

City Bounty Contests aren't a standalone feature – they're deeply integrated with {siteName}'s core systems:

### Points System Integration

Trees planted during contests still cost and earn [ecosystem points](/blog/mitimara-dual-point-system) as normal, with the contest rewards being additional bonuses. This ensures continuous engagement with our core platform.

### Verification Process

All contest trees undergo the [standard community verification process](/blog/how-to-verify-a-tree), maintaining platform integrity while allowing for the additional reward layer.

### Community Policing

Our [policing system](/blog/introducing-mitimara-community-policing) applies to contest trees as well, ensuring that the increased incentives don't lead to increased fraud attempts.

## Conclusion: Local Action, Global Vision

City Bounty Contests represent {siteName}'s commitment to making environmental action both impactful and engaging. By adding a competitive, gamified layer to our core tree-planting mission, we're creating stronger incentives for users to take action where it's needed most.

Whether you're planting your first tree or your hundredth, bounty contests provide an exciting way to maximize your impact, connect with your community, and earn rewards for positive environmental action.

The global challenge of climate change requires solutions that work at every level – from individual actions to community initiatives to global movements. City Bounty Contests bridge these scales, turning local action into global impact, one tree at a time.
